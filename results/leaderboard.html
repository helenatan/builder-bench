📊 The signal: a dead heat — the flagship isn't better, it's differently good

All three tiers solve the same number of tasks (80%), but not the same tasks. When a task names what to do, every tier ships it. On the hard cases they trade failures: Opus alone clarifies the vague ask (a-05) and catches the outlier (d-07), but Opus alone over-builds (a-01) and mis-reports the truncated count (d-05). Only the structural-duplication trap (d-02) defeats everyone. The budget tier ties the flagship at ~¼ the cost-per-solved.
